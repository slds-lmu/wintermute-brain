## Summary

The staff profile of Jonas Müller at the Lehrstuhl für Maschinelles Lernen
und Datenanalytik (Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab), Department
Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical Engineering (AIBE), FAU Erlangen. It
lists his CV, research projects, publications, teaching and supervised
theses [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).

## Key points

- **Position:** PhD candidate at the Machine Learning and Data Analytics
  Lab since 11/2023 [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
- **Education:** M.Sc. in Cognitive Science, University of Tübingen
  (11/2020-06/2023; thesis "Glacier Movement Prediction with Deep Learning
  Models and Satellite Data"), and B.Sc. in Psychology, Alpen-Adria-University
  (10/2017-06/2020) [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
- **Earlier roles:** Artificial Intelligence Expert at Bosch-Siemens Home
  Appliances GmbH (05/2022-02/2023), an internship at the Human Machine
  Cognition Lab, Tübingen, on evolutionary algorithms and Bayesian
  reinforcement learning, and scientific assistant positions in Tübingen
  [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
- **Projects:** PreHapp (a motion-tracking module for a rehabilitation
  application using deep learning and computer vision) and EmpkinS
  [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
- **Publications** centre on human pose estimation from millimeter wave
  radar (e.g. "RadProPoser: Probabilistic Radar Tensor Human Pose Estimation
  That Knows Its Limits", IJCNN 2026), digital physiotherapy ("Adaptive
  Biofeedback for Digital Physiotherapy Using Sakoe-Chiba Constrained Pose
  Matching"), cross-model invariances ("Understanding cross-model perceptual
  invariances through ensemble metamers", Neural Computing & Applications)
  and glacier movement prediction (NeurIPS 2023 workshop). The extracted
  page drops most co-author names
  [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
- **Teaching:** Scrum Master in the Innovation Lab for Wearable and
  Ubiquitous Computing; co-supervised a 2023 Master's thesis on measuring
  invariances between models using model metamers
  [Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
