## Summary

The staff profile of Thomas Altstidl at the Lehrstuhl für Maschinelles Lernen
und Datenanalytik (Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab), Department
Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical Engineering (AIBE), FAU Erlangen. It
lists his CV, research projects, publications, teaching and supervised
theses [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).

## Key points

- **Position:** Researcher & PhD Candidate; Ph.D. student at the Machine
  Learning and Data Analytics Lab since 04/2021
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Education:** M.Sc. in Computer Science at FAU (10/2018 – 03/2021; Master
  thesis “Scale-invariant Convolution Kernels”) and B.Sc. in Computer Science
  at FAU (10/2015 – 04/2019; Bachelor thesis “Application of Deep Learning
  Methods to Process Natural Phenomena”)
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Earlier roles:** student research assistant at the Machine Learning and
  Data Analytics Lab (04/2020 – 06/2020) and at the Fraunhofer Society,
  Precise Positioning and Analytics Department; student teaching assistant
  in FAU's Distributed Systems and Operating Systems Group
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Projects:** DigiOnko is the only research project listed; the profile
  does not describe his role in it
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Publications (2021 to 2025)** cover several lines of work. Scale
  equivariance: "Just a Matter of Scale? Reevaluating Scale Equivariance in
  Convolutional Neural Networks" (IJCNN 2023). Clustering comparison:
  "p-value Adjustment for Monotonous, Unbiased, and Fast Clustering
  Comparison" (Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 36) and
  "The Impact of Random Models on Standardized Clustering Similarity" (IEEE
  Access). Adversarial robustness: "On the Scalability of Certified
  Adversarial Robustness with Generated Data" (Advances in Neural Information
  Processing Systems 37) and "Large-Scale Dataset Pruning in Adversarial
  Training through Data Importance Extrapolation" (ICML 2024 DMLR workshop).
  Radio localization: "Accuracy-Aware Compression of Channel Impulse
  Responses using Deep Learning" (IPIN 2021) and "Non-Line-of-Sight Detection
  for Radio Localization using Deep State Space Models" (IPIN 2024). The
  extracted page drops the co-author names, so it does not show which papers
  he leads
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Further publications** are on multimodal fusion for mixed-type time
  series (IJCNN 2024), zero-shot human scanpath prediction with contrastive
  language-image pretrained models (IEEE Transactions on Artificial
  Intelligence), and biomedical applications: fetal re-identification in
  multiple pregnancy ultrasound images (EMBC 2023), smartphone auscultation
  for severe aortic stenosis detection (HICSS 2025), survival and risk
  assessment in preclinical rheumatoid arthritis (IEEE Journal of Biomedical
  and Health Informatics) and in vivo skin-type classification with
  millimeter-wave near-field probe spectroscopy (European Microwave
  Conference 2022)
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Teaching:** Machine Learning for Engineers I and II in every semester
  listed, from SS 2021 to WS 2024/25
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
- **Supervised theses:** ten Bachelor and Master theses between 07/2021 and
  12/2023, on topics such as mixed-type time series, monocular depth
  estimation, fetal re-identification, compression of wideband channel
  measurements, disease progression in multiple sclerosis, and
  non-line-of-sight detection for radio localisation; some with another main
  supervisor or co-supervisor
  [Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
