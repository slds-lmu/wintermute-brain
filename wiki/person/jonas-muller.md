## Expertise

Human pose estimation from millimeter wave radar data, including
probabilistic models with uncertainty; pose matching for digital
physiotherapy biofeedback; perceptual invariances across models (model
metamers); earlier work on glacier movement prediction from satellite data
and on evolutionary and Bayesian reinforcement learning. Background in
cognitive science (M.Sc., Tübingen) and psychology (B.Sc.)
[Jonas Müller profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-jonas-muller-fau-profile.md).
