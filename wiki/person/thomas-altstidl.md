## Expertise

Scale equivariance and scale-invariant kernels in convolutional neural
networks; clustering comparison measures (p-value adjustment, standardized
clustering similarity); certified adversarial robustness with generated data
and dataset pruning for adversarial training; deep learning for radio
localization (non-line-of-sight detection, compression of channel impulse
responses); multimodal fusion for mixed-type time series. Applied work in
biomedical settings: fetal re-identification in ultrasound images, smartphone
auscultation for aortic stenosis detection, risk assessment in preclinical
rheumatoid arthritis, and skin-type classification with millimeter-wave
spectroscopy. Background in computer science (M.Sc. and B.Sc., FAU)
[Thomas Altstidl profile](/wiki/source/src-20261105-thomas-altstidl-fau-profile.md).
